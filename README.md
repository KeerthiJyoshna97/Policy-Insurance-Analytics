# Policy Insurance Analytics

An Excel-based Insurance Analytics project focused on analyzing policy, customer, claims, and payment data to generate business insights through interactive dashboards and KPI reporting.

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes an insurance dataset containing **5,000 policy records** along with customer, claims, payment, risk, and policy-related information.

The analysis focuses on understanding policy participation, policy status, customer demographics, insurance types, premium trends, and policy-ending patterns.

## 📌 Key Analysis Areas

- Policy status and active/inactive policy analysis
- Age-group-wise policy participation
- Gender-wise policy participation
- Policy-type analysis

## 🔍 Key Insights

- The dataset contains **5,000 customers and 5,000 policies**.
- **1,682 policies (33.64%)** are active, while **3,318 policies (66.36%)** are inactive.
- The **60–75 age group** has the highest policy participation.
- **Health insurance** has the highest policy count among the analyzed policy types.
- Policy participation is relatively balanced across genders.
- Premium growth fluctuates across the analyzed years.
- Policy endings show significant variation over time, with a peak in **2025**.
